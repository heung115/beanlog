## Evidence limits and selection rules

- Three adopted PRESS/Methodical pages were fully readable as official-domain indexed results but returned cache miss on `web.open`. Ordinary public HTTP fallback from the shell could not resolve hosts inside its network sandbox. These are indexed-primary evidence, not falsely claimed successful live HTTP reads; the full identity, regional, cultivar, process and notes fields were available in search output.
- No harvested year was inferred from copyright, publication, roast date, retail URL, filename, “new”, or stock status.
- ONA's Batian notes are a milk-roast expression. They stay on its specific named selection; importer cupping notes and regional taste summaries were not overwritten.
- Existing unknown fields cannot be patched from another farm, a general cultivar essay, or village tree inventory. Soe Ae's explicit new processor/crop/flavour evidence is useful despite its unpublished lot cultivar.
- No added lot is assigned a quality score. Roaster descriptions of genetic parentage, universal regional flavours, guarantees or certifications were not copied.
