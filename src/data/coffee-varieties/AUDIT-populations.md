# Additional population and cultivar audit

Checked 2026-09-09. Scope: previously unlinked Harrar, Burundi, India Robusta, Vietnam, Yemen and Espírito Santo labels. Only `populations.ts` and this audit are owned by this addition. Fourteen entries; exact aliases only.

## Decisions

- Ethiopian heirloom is a collective trade term, not a cultivar. Keffa's glossary is direct importer terminology; its numerical genetic-diversity and flavor claims are omitted. JARC selections and individual local landrace names are not aliases.
- Red Bourbon describes red-fruited Bourbon material. It is not linked to a Brazilian IAC accession, Yellow Bourbon, Pink Bourbon or every Local Bourbon label. Sucafina's Rwanda subsidiary and Guatemala sourcing pages supply actual cultivation examples. No universal flavor or disease-resistance claim.
- S274 and CxR remain separate. WCR's Sln.1R page currently mixes a CxR alias and interspecific lineage with S274's selection history. Only its S274 history is used; Coffee Board regional lists and the WCR/CCRI/WASI researcher-authored 2026 review distinguish Sln.1R (S274) from Sln.3R (CxR). No Sln.1R alias is added because the clone versus seedling-progeny usage needs finer treatment.
- CxR ancestry and compactness come from the breeder-authored review, section 2.2 and Table 3. It is not a Coffea arabica cultivar. The cited article was published 15 January 2026 despite its DOI containing 2025.
- Peridenia is retained in Coffee Board's spelling and described only through its Robusta cultivation lists. WCR's Perdenia page gives a different spelling; those labels were not merged just from resemblance. WCR's additional morphology and introduction narrative were not transferred.
- TR4 is a WASI Robusta clone. WCR documents a Dak Lak mother tree, 2006 approval and grafting.
- Yemen entries use firsthand farmer interviews in Montagnon et al. (2022), Table 3. The study found no significant name/genetic-group association; consequently each entry describes the name's origin, never a unique genotype or a guaranteed physical phenotype. Jaadi/Jadi are explicitly connected in Table 3. Tufahi, Jufaini, Udaini and Dawairi retain the study spellings.
- Catuaí Vermelho is a red-fruited cultivar group, not all Catuaí. Incaper's official handbook search extraction supports parentage and 1972 release; its direct PDF opening failed. IAC's current cultivar register independently supports release and compact stature. No unconfirmed performance detail is used.
- Catucaí 785 is kept at lineage scope: the Procafé breeder's 2023 technical account explicitly distinguishes red and yellow 785-15 material. No color-specific resistance, yield or architecture is assigned to the unqualified 785 label. The article is authored by J. B. Matiello and Ubiratan V. Barros and republished by CCCMG.

## Intentionally unresolved

Cà Sẻ, Local Bourbon, Dawaery, Dawayri, Udain, Tuffahi, Jufini and Jaa’di were not automatically equated to another entry. Alternate transliteration and farmer naming can be meaningful; an actual source connecting the exact labels is required. The provided source's Jadi Arabic gloss supports that specific alias only. These omissions do not block other documented varieties in the same regions from displaying details.

## Provenance

All public entries retain direct URLs and checked dates. Sources shared across entries are kept concise in aggregate, including both languages. This audit adds editorial decisions rather than additional copied source passages. The Coffee Board regional source is shared with `regional.ts`; its combined public descriptions should remain below the 200-word source budget after integration.
